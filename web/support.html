Model Tracer v1.6.4

Support

Model Tracer is an Excel Office Add-in for formula tracing, financial-model formatting and workbook audit checks.

End-user support

If the add-in was deployed by your organisation and does not appear in Excel, restart Excel first. If it remains unavailable, contact your Microsoft 365 / desktop support team and provide the add-in name Model Tracer.

Network requirements

The deployed add-in needs access to this approved hosting origin and to Microsoft's Office.js CDN at https://appsforoffice.microsoft.com.

Privacy

This build has no application backend, analytics or telemetry. See Privacy & data handling.
