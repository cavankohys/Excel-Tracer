Model Tracer - Terms of Use

Internal deployment template. The deploying organisation should review and replace this page with its approved internal terms before production use.

Purpose

Model Tracer is an Excel productivity add-in for formula exploration, financial-model formatting and workbook audit review.

Model review

Audit findings are review indicators rather than a substitute for professional judgement or independent model verification.

Organisation policy

Use remains subject to the deploying organisation's information-security, acceptable-use and data-handling policies.
